Configure Stripe's secret key and webhook signing secret in the server-side Taon environment ({{ context.codeblock1 }} and {{ context.codeblock2 }}). Never expose these values to browser code. The Products page's Sync with Stripe dialog checks the server connection, synchronizes local products, and displays the dedicated webhook URL to register in Stripe.

Stripe webhooks are signature-verified against the raw request body. Orders and product entitlements are finalized from verified Stripe events, not from the browser success redirect. Day-based subscription intervals use Stripe recurring prices; seconds, minutes, and hours are represented as Taon-side entitlement expirations.

{{ context.codeblock14 }} is also accepted for existing Stripe configurations. Handlers retrieve current Stripe resources rather than trusting an older event's snapshot. Local webhook writes use serializable transactions, so failed processing rolls back and Stripe can retry. Checkout, subscription and invoice IDs identify existing records; replaying a checkout neither consumes a new cart nor extends a fixed-duration entitlement.

Expired checkouts cancel pending orders and release stock/purchase reservations. Cart items remain available for another checkout. Leaving Stripe through {{ context.codeblock15 }} only displays a message; it does not cancel the order.

Canceling subscription renewal retains access through the paid period end; that deadline is enforced even if the final cancellation webhook is delayed. Actual cancellation, unpaid, paused and expired states remove access. {{ context.codeblock16 }} retains access during Stripe's retry/dunning window; configure Stripe's final failed-payment action to cancel or mark the subscription unpaid when that window ends. Invoices update payment history and reconcile the current subscription period and status, including when delivered before checkout completion.

Full refunds and full-amount lost disputes for normal/lifetime Checkout payments mark the order/payment refunded and revoke that order's entitlements. Partial refunds and partial lost disputes leave access intact. Other purchases and directly assigned permissions are not revoked. A refunded lifetime purchase can be purchased again.

Product presentation and eligibility

Use the product editor's Link to post field to associate a CMS post. Published post titles, excerpts, public content and thumbnails enrich the product details dialog. Protected post bodies and video/audio/file attachments are not exposed by the sales preview. Product thumbnails reuse CMS storage/download tokens, including for posts whose full content requires purchase.

Product information opens a normal details dialog from Buy, Cart and Checkout; purchase, quantity and remove controls are separate. List thumbnails are bounded to 100px height, checkout thumbnails to 50px, preserving aspect ratio. Neither list downloads full post media attachments.

The backend exposes {{ context.codeblock17 }} on suggested products. Its shared eligibility check is also used by cart mutations and checkout, enforcing singleton lifetime/subscription purchases, ownership/current subscriptions, pending reservations, stock, per-user limits, currency and recurring-checkout compatibility. Unavailable suggestions remain visible with a reason; cart changes refresh their eligibility. Lifetime and subscription cart quantities are always one.
