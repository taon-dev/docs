Marketing-related analytics events

The marketing-related capability documented here is the generic event collection in {{ context.codeblock0 }}. It lets an application record interactions such as a promotion or banner click. Analytics does not provide campaign planning, email marketing, audience segmentation, or a marketing automation engine.
